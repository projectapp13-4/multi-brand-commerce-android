# Privacy and release operating contract

Status: **PRE-P3-16 OWNERSHIP CONTRACT READY — ACTION-TIME ASSIGNMENTS AND PRODUCTION DECISIONS PENDING** (2026-09-26).

This is the current role and decision contract for Gürbakır's intended Türkiye-only Android launch. It does not assign a person, start P3-16, approve a legal policy, submit Play declarations, provision production services, or promise completion of an individual erasure case. Historical P3-13/P3-15 evidence keeps its checkpoint scope. The [pre-P3-16 deletion continuation](../phase3/PRE-P3-16-ACCOUNT-DELETION-ROUTE-AND-PROCESS.md) records the operational handoff witness; [Brand Boundaries](../architecture/BRAND-BOUNDARIES.md) defines Shopify-aligned ownership.

## Operational ownership boundary

| Ownership class | Accountable scope |
|---|---|
| Merchant / brand | Shopify store administration, products/orders/fulfillment/refunds, merchant legal and privacy policy, first-line customer support, privacy/deletion intake and decisions, Shopify erasure execution, processor/retention decisions, pixels/marketing/privacy settings, and merchant website/domain content. |
| App provider / engineering | Native Android code and UX, architecture, security/dependency maintenance, CI/build engineering, technical testing and evidence, integration implementation, application maintenance, release artifact engineering, and technical incident investigation/remediation. |
| Deployment-model-dependent | Play publisher and release-track operation, upload-key custody, Firebase administration, domain/App Links deployment coordination, and Play listing/Data Safety operational submission. The brand onboarding record must choose the accountable side and access for each; runtime Android architecture does not change with that choice. |

For this Gürbakır deployment, Shopify policy/support/privacy and the domain remain merchant-owned; Android engineering and maintenance are project-managed. The existing project-managed Play account is the intended publisher, and Play release operation and upload-key custody are project-managed. Firebase production administration remains a decision tied to the final provider graph. The app provider coordinates technical App Links association with the merchant domain owner. No individual is inferred from account access or this contract. Merchant-facing support remains the first contact for brand, account, order, privacy and policy matters; technical issues escalate to the app provider.

## Visitor privacy product decision

**NO SOURCE CHANGE REQUIRED — SHOPIFY-ALIGNED HOSTED CONTROLS + INTENTIONAL VISITORCONSENT OMISSION.** This is the current product default for the observed Türkiye-only merchant setup, subject to final policy and production-artifact review before public release.

The only active Shopify market is Türkiye. Current Admin `consentPolicy(TR)` returns `consentRequired=false` and `dataSaleOptOutRequired=false`. The merchant's hosted privacy policy is published; its cookie banner is configured for 31 other regions, not Türkiye, with automatic banner settings off and checkout banner display off. Shopify Network Intelligence is enabled. Customer events shows one Google & YouTube **Web** app pixel with optimized access; its displayed classification says consent is not required and data collection is not classified as data sale. Server pixel delivery is **UNKNOWN** because this connector lacks the required scopes and the visible Web list cannot establish it.

Current Android source collects no purpose-specific privacy preference. `CartCreate.graphql` omits Storefront `visitorConsent`; Checkout Kit's `onWebPixelEvent` is a no-op. Analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled/absent in the current release graph; Messaging is debug-only. These source facts require revalidation against the final production artifact and service bindings. Shopify's [browser Customer Privacy API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/customer-privacy) applies to Shopify-managed surfaces. Its [mobile Checkout Kit guide](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/mobile/checkout-kit/privacy-compliance) describes passing a choice **collected by the app** through Storefront context; the reviewed contracts do not document a native read/sync of a visitor's hosted choice. This is a bounded documentation inference. [VisitorConsent](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/input-objects/VisitorConsent) omission/null means no app-provided preference, neither consent nor refusal. Checkout Kit does not require invented booleans when the app has collected no choice.

Hosted/site choices are not silently copied into Android. Shopify controls its hosted site/account/checkout surfaces according to their applicable settings; Android truthfully omits a choice it did not collect. Network Intelligence and pixel activity still require accurate merchant privacy-policy and draft Play Data Safety mapping. Shopify says integrated site choices affect Network Intelligence and that [app pixels may transmit through web or server paths](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/pixels/app-pixels); no conclusion about the unknown server path is inferred. [Customer Privacy settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/customer-privacy-settings/privacy-settings) must be reviewed when the merchant changes them.

Reopen this decision before adding a launch market, changing merchant consent policy, adding/changing a pixel or server integration, enabling Analytics/Crashlytics/ads or production Messaging, collecting Android marketing/privacy preferences, or when Shopify/Play contracts change. If Android later collects a choice, open a separate source campaign for purpose-specific tri-state state, accessible UX, secure persistence/restoration and withdrawal, Storefront context propagation, fresh checkout URL, preload invalidation, Trial/Synthetic isolation, tests and revised disclosures. No such source work is authorized by this contract.

## Privacy and deletion operations

The merchant-owned **Merchant Privacy Operations** function owns intake, a restricted case record, subject-control verification, acknowledgement, processor/retention checklist, authorized Shopify erasure or eligible profile action, provider readback, status and outcome notices, duplicates, uncertain results and escalation. It needs scoped merchant inbox/case access and the Shopify customer/privacy permissions necessary for its assigned actions. The app provider implements and maintains the request route and supplies technical escalation evidence; it does not silently become the merchant's case operator. An app/browser return never proves request submission.

The merchant-owned **Privacy / Policy Steward** owns the merchant privacy-policy version, Shopify Customer Privacy, pixel and Network Intelligence review, market-change review, Play Data Safety policy input and visitor-consent reopening. Merchant **Customer Support Escalation** owns first-line account/order/privacy and deletion-status questions, unresolved processor cases and technical-incident referral. The app provider owns technical auth/app remediation and supplies the artifact/provider facts needed for accurate policy and Data Safety decisions. One accountable function may fill multiple roles after access and backup coverage are explicitly recorded; no individual is assigned here.

The restricted case system must retain only the minimum operational facts: pseudonymous case reference; intake channel/time; verification method and result; authorized operator; acknowledgement evidence; relevant processor and retention checklist with basis and review owner; provider action receipt/readback and pending state; exception or failure; notice state; closure decision; access/retention record. Customer identifiers, message bodies, raw provider payloads and identity evidence remain in access-controlled merchant systems only. Never put them in Git, CI, app logs, analytics, public issue comments or this document. A case reference in Git must not identify a customer. Apply the merchant's approved retention schedule to the restricted record; this contract invents no fixed duration.

| Case state | Required action / truth boundary |
|---|---|
| Received / verification pending | Record intake and verify control before account-specific action or disclosure. |
| Acknowledged | Confirm receipt and next step without promising an unsupported deadline or completed deletion. |
| Processor and retention review | Identify Shopify, installed apps/channels and other companies; record any legitimate retention exception for policy review. |
| Provider submitted / pending | Record one authorized request and its receipt; read back status. A timeout or duplicate is **unknown outcome** until reconciled, not permission for blind resubmission. |
| Partial retention / exception | Explain the supported residual data category and review path under approved policy; do not claim blanket erasure. |
| Completed and notified | Close only after the required provider/processor readback and a truthful requester notice. |
| Escalated / unable to verify | Track ownership, reason, next review and safe user-facing response; avoid disclosing another subject's data. |

Escalate identity mismatch, order/subscription/retention complexity, failed or uncertain Shopify/processor action, overdue response under the merchant's **approved** policy, contradictory provider readback, inaccessible intake, or a request that alleges a privacy incident. Use communication categories of receipt, verification needed, provider pending, partial retention, completed, and escalation. The public deletion resource and support route must state how to request and follow up, what can remain, and what to expect without inventing days. [Shopify's erasure workflow](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/processing-customer-data-requests) is asynchronous and distinct from profile deletion; the synthetic MPA-B schedule is case follow-through, not a P3-16 gate. [Google Play's account-deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en) requires an in-app and public web request path and asks developers to describe expectations and legitimate retention truthfully.

## Release, incident and recovery roles

| Function | Contract and required access |
|---|---|
| Release Operator | Deployment owner coordinates approved Play track/upload, while app-provider engineering builds exact approved source and verifies protected CI and release evidence. For Gürbakır this is project-managed. Needs scoped repository/CI and Play release access; never bypasses protection. |
| Incident Coordinator | App provider classifies and remediates technical auth, cart, checkout, migration, App Links, outage and provider incidents; merchant support/privacy owners retain customer communication, commerce decisions and privacy cases. Coordinates both sides and records provider impact. |
| Rollback / Recovery Authority | Deployment owner decides Play halt, safe fail-forward or higher-version recovery with app-provider evidence and merchant support coordination. For Gürbakır this is project-managed. The first Play release has no prior app version to roll back to; halting an update does not remove it from devices already updated. Never promise a downgrade of migrated local data. |
| Signing Custodian | Deployment owner holds the separately approved upload-key material, backup/recovery procedure, limited access and key-use receipts. For Gürbakır this is project-managed. The custody role does not imply another brand must use the same model. |
| Release Evidence Custodian | App provider retains source SHA, AAB/APK hash, certificate fingerprint, R8 mapping, SBOM/licenses, provider receipts, CI/device evidence, Play track/readback and recovery record in access-controlled storage. May combine with Release Operator if access and independent review are adequate. |

The merchant public support contact receives brand/account/order/privacy questions first, routes deletion cases to Merchant Privacy Operations, and escalates technical auth/app/security failures to the app-provider Incident Coordinator. This uses the existing owned support channel, not a new backend or a transfer of merchant policy responsibility.

Before upload, stop and review a failed/unknown auth or callback route, checkout/payment path, cart ownership/integrity, deletion intake or status handling, signing/artifact identity, local migration, or App Links association. During a rollout, halt expansion and triage those failures, provider outages and significant crash/ANR regressions. Capture affected version, market, provider, timestamps, safe reproduction and rollback choice without customer secrets. [Play's Android vitals](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844486?hl=en) currently defines overall user-perceived crash and ANR bad-behavior thresholds of 1.09% and 0.47% of daily users, with 8% per-device thresholds; use those as platform signals, not a fabricated project SLO. Project alert and rollout thresholds are **SET DURING P3-16 AFTER PRODUCTION/TEST-TRACK BASELINE**.

[Play staged rollouts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6346149?hl=en) apply to **updates**, not the first publication. Halting stops additional eligible users but leaves already updated devices on that version. A first release therefore needs prelaunch testing, a halt/unpublish and support decision, and a higher-version recovery path rather than an assumed previous binary. Later releases may use a staged percentage chosen in P3-16 from the actual track, baseline and incident coverage. No country or percentage is chosen here. Source revert alone is not a device rollback; migration compatibility and a signed higher-version fix require proof.

## Assignment and action timing

The [P3-16 roadmap](../phase3/PHASE-3-IMPLEMENTATION-ROADMAP.md) requires support and release ownership. The three ownership classes above identify accountable *sides* for technical preflight without inventing project-internal merchant staff. The merchant must designate its actual privacy/policy/support access and coverage before a closed test can generate live requests or user-facing support needs. The project-managed deployment must designate release, technical-incident and recovery access before Play upload/test rollout; Signing Custodian before key creation/use and Release Evidence Custodian before a production candidate is built. A person may hold multiple compatible functions with explicit coverage and least privilege. Actual publication, key use/replacement, irreversible production changes and real-customer actions retain the [owner policy's](../OWNER-AUTHORITY-AND-APPROVAL-BOUNDARIES.md) point-of-action boundaries. Independent read-only design and preparation do not depend on naming people.
